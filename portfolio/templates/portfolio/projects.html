RayJolt Pool

A standard poolhall billiards game I made using open source projects on github. For the renderer I used Raylib, for the physics I used Jolt Physics, I also used EnTT for an ECS system to provide the game architecture, ImGui for debugging and quick menus, and finally ENet as a quick UDP base for the multiplayer networking.

TCMG!

The CEO Must Go! You've just been laid off. Retaliate with chaos and fun in this physics based game. Confront the CEO by moving up the office building and use anything you can pick up to buy you more time before you're escorted out. Don't sell out, buy in the fun. Developed in Unreal Engine 5.

Driving To Grandmas

A management game about traveling across the country to your mother's house. You play as a father on a road trip with his family. You will make decisions about where to stop on the way, what to do there and play minigames to appease the needs of your family members. If you fail it may just spell the end of married life. Developed in Unreal 5.

Containment Breach

A fast paced shooter where you are a parasite with no weapons. Luckily you can inhabit those that do. Can you navigate the spaceport you've been brought to and escape to freedom? Developed in Unity 6.

Dreamline

Dreamline is a group project developed with a focus on accessibility. Our challenge was to design and build a game using the Xbox Adaptive Controller, ensuring the experience could be enjoyed with alternative input methods. Developed in Unity 6.
